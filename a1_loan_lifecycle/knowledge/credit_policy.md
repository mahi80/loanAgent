# Corporate Credit Policy (SYNTHETIC — for prototype only)

## CP-2.1 Sanctions
Any confirmed match against OFAC, UN, EU or HMT sanctions lists results in mandatory decline. No exception authority exists.

## CP-2.2 Politically Exposed Persons
Where a director, shareholder or beneficial owner is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is mandatory and approval must be escalated one level above the normal authority.

## CP-2.3 Adverse Media
Credible adverse media relating to fraud, corruption, bribery or regulatory investigation of the borrower or sponsor group requires referral to Compliance before any credit decision.

## CP-2.4 Beneficial Ownership
A signed beneficial owner declaration is mandatory for all borrowers before sanction. Applications without it are incomplete.

## CP-3.1 Vintage
Borrowers must have a minimum of 3 years of operating history. Project SPVs younger than 3 years require a sponsor corporate guarantee.

## CP-3.3 Audited Financials
For exposures above USD 1,000,000 audited financial statements for the latest financial year are mandatory. Management accounts are acceptable only for interim periods. A qualified audit opinion must be explained in the credit memo.

## CP-4.1 Debt Service Coverage
Pro-forma Debt Service Coverage Ratio (EBITDA / total annual debt service including the proposed facility) must be at least 1.25x. Between 1.10x and 1.25x is permitted only with additional collateral and Credit Committee approval.

## CP-4.2 Leverage
Pro-forma Total Debt / EBITDA must not exceed 4.0x for corporate loans. Project finance may go to 6.0x during construction with a sponsor completion guarantee.

## CP-4.3 Loan to Value
For secured facilities, Loan-to-Value must not exceed 70% of the independent collateral valuation.

## CP-4.4 Collateral Valuation
Collateral must be valued by a bank-empanelled independent valuer. Valuations older than 12 months are stale and must be refreshed before disbursement.

## CP-5.1 Sector Watchlist
Infrastructure (toll roads, power) and commercial real estate are watchlist sectors. Exposures require sector-head concurrence and stress testing of cash flows at -20% revenue.

## CP-5.2 Account Conduct
More than 3 cheque or ECS bounces in the last 12 months, or peak overdraft utilisation above 90%, indicates cash-flow stress and must be flagged as a risk observation.

## CP-5.3 Customer Concentration
Where a single customer exceeds 25% of revenue the concentration must be mitigated (assignment of receivables or covenant).

## CP-6.1 Approval Authority Matrix
Up to USD 2,000,000: Credit Manager. Up to USD 10,000,000: Senior Credit Officer. Up to USD 20,000,000: Credit Committee. Above USD 20,000,000: Board Credit Committee. Any policy exception escalates one level.

## CP-7.1 Conditions Precedent to Disbursement
Before first disbursement: executed facility agreement, perfected security (charge registration), insurance of collateral with bank as loss payee, board resolution of borrower, and fresh valuation if older than 12 months.

## CP-8.1 Monitoring Covenants
Financial covenants (DSCR, leverage) are tested quarterly. Headroom below 10% to any covenant, overdue above 30 days, or two consecutive quarters of DSCR decline triggers an Early Warning Signal (EWS) review.
